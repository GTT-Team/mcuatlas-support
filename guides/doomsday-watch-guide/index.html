Guide 01 / Catch up

What to watch before Avengers: Doomsday

Short on time? For returning fans, I’d start with Avengers: Infinity War, then Avengers: Endgame, then The Fantastic Four: First Steps. It gives you the previous Avengers payoff and introduces the Fantastic Four without turning December into homework.

This is an independent, spoiler-light recommendation, not a list of confirmed prerequisites. Disney lists Avengers: Doomsday for U.S. theaters on December 18, 2026 and describes heroes from three universes coming together. That makes a few focused viewing routes more useful than an exhaustive chronological marathon.

The three-film refresher

  1. Avengers: Infinity War (2018). Start here for Thanos and the Infinity Stones. It’s the first half of the Avengers story you’re about to revisit.
  2. Avengers: Endgame (2019). Watch immediately afterward. It concludes that two-film arc and gives the era’s Avengers roster a clear point of reference.
  3. The Fantastic Four: First Steps (2025). Meet Reed Richards, Sue Storm, Johnny Storm and Ben Grimm on their own terms. Marvel introduces them in a retro-futuristic setting, not as a side story in a previous Avengers movie.

Pick one optional detour

For the returning X-Men: X-Men (2000)

Go back to the original live-action team for Professor X, Magneto and Cyclops. Disney has confirmed returning performers including Patrick Stewart and Ian McKellen. Watch for familiarity with the cast and characters, not because the earlier movie explains precisely how Doomsday connects its universes.

For the multiverse: Loki, seasons 1 and 2

Start at season one, episode one. Follow the Time Variance Authority story in sequence instead of trying to slot individual episodes around the films. It’s an optional route for the vocabulary of branches and timelines, not a confirmed viewing requirement.

For Wanda and Vision: WandaVision → Agatha All Along → VisionQuest

Disney describes these three series as one trilogy. Watch them in that order if you’re specifically following the Wanda and Vision side of the MCU. This is a separate character route; it’s not evidence that the series are necessary to understand Doomsday.

What if I’ve never watched Marvel?

I’d begin with Iron Man and the early team-building films before jumping into Infinity War. If you only have one evening and want a movie that introduces its own team, choose First Steps. For more options, see MCU release order vs. timeline order.
